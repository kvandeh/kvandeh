Today I sent a message to more than 2,500 users of [PeekSeek](https://peekseek.net). It told them that their data will be deleted. I am shutting PeekSeek down, because it costs more money and time than I can keep putting into it.

I loved doing it. This is how it went.

## 2024: an idea

::: timeline
- Nov 2024 | An idea called "unturned.io" becomes a real project. The Discord server opens on 21 November.
- Dec 2024 | The first version works. In the first playtest the website held up, and the game server broke the moment it heard about teams.
:::

Unturned had no matchmaking. If you wanted a fair fight, you found one yourself. PeekSeek was the button that did it for you: log in with Steam, press "Find Match", play.

## 2025: a flop, then a comeback

::: timeline
- Jan 2025 | ! We talk to Smartly Dressed Games, the studio behind Unturned, and host an **official event**: the UCL Winter Cup.
- Feb 2025 | It flopped terribly. I apologise in public and hand tournament leadership to someone else.
:::

The first event went wrong in almost every way it could. Afterwards we decided that tournaments would run on points, inside the normal queue, automated by the platform. So I kept building.

::: timeline
- Jul 2025 | Solo queue launches for everyone.
- Aug 2025 | ! 16 servers worldwide and the AMOX Studios Summer Cup 2025, **one of the biggest and most successful events** the scene had seen.
- Sep 2025 | 1,928 matches across 348 users. Then activity drops.
- Oct 2025 | The Asia region opens.
:::

That September I tried to hand PeekSeek to someone in the community, without success. I asked for support instead: five euros paid for two game servers for a month. It was enough to continue.

## 2026: four continents

::: timeline
- Feb 2026 | ! The Winter Cup 2026, our **highest production value yet**, commentated live. First events for Asia, North America and South America.
- Apr 2026 | A second map, Washington Arena.
- Jul 2026 | The first line of PeekSeek written with AI. Everything before it, I wrote by hand.
- Aug 2026 | ! The TopUnturned.com **3v3 Summer Cup final**: six teams.
- Sep 2026 | Seasons, ranks and RAID, a new game mode with building and explosives.
- Oct 2026 | ! PeekSeek becomes **open source**.
:::

By now PeekSeek was more than a queue. Sponsors paid for prizes and servers: AMOX Studios, TopUnturned.com, MountHost, FangHosting, PineHosting and ReTake Events. All store earnings still went to prize money. In total, players finished more than 10,000 matches.

## The end

::: timeline
- Nov 2026 | The shutdown notice. Data requests close on 28 November, and all data is wiped on 1 December.
:::

PeekSeek never cost much in euros. But each event needed rules, referees, servers in the right region, a stream and someone to answer questions at midnight. For two years, that someone was me.

The code stays online at [github.com/PeekSeek/PeekSeek](https://github.com/PeekSeek/PeekSeek), with a guide to host your own. If someone wants to run the scene again, everything they need is public.

## What it taught me

I wrote PeekSeek by hand for its first twenty months. It taught me to keep things flexible. PeekSeek moved hosts twice, the event format changed after the flop, and Unturned kept changing under us.
