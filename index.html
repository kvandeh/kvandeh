Personal site of Kieran van der Heijde, a Python Engineer at ASML in Veldhoven, the Netherlands. He works mostly in Python with Flask and FastAPI. He built and runs PeekSeek, a competitive matchmaking platform for the game Unturned with more than 2,000 users and 10,000 matches, officially partnered with Smartly Dressed Games, and AlgoStudio, a platform for teaching programming with sandboxed Python and C#.
